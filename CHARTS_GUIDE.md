# 📊 Charts & Analytics Guide

## Overview

The dashboard now displays **real-time data** from your receipts, organized by **category** and **status**. All charts update automatically when you create, edit, or delete receipts.

---

## 📈 Available Charts

### 1. Items by Category (Pie Chart)
**Location**: Top Left

**What it shows**: Distribution of items across different categories

**Data Source**: 
- Counts the total quantity of items in each category
- Categories: Electronics, Food, Clothing, Furniture, Office Supplies, Other

**Use Case**: 
- See which product categories you sell most
- Identify your top-performing categories
- Balance inventory across categories

**Example**:
- Electronics: 45 items
- Food: 32 items
- Clothing: 28 items

---

### 2. Revenue by Category (Bar Chart)
**Location**: Top Right

**What it shows**: Total revenue generated by each category

**Data Source**:
- Sums the net amount of all items in each category
- Shows dollar amounts on Y-axis
- Categories on X-axis

**Use Case**:
- Identify which categories generate most revenue
- Compare profitability across categories
- Make informed inventory decisions

**Example**:
- Electronics: $4,500
- Furniture: $3,200
- Food: $1,800

---

### 3. Monthly Receipts by Status (Line Chart)
**Location**: Bottom Left

**What it shows**: Trend of receipts over the last 6 months, broken down by status

**Data Source**:
- Groups receipts by month
- Separates by status: Draft, Pending, Paid, Cancelled
- Shows last 6 months of data

**Color Coding**:
- 🔵 **Draft** - Blue line
- 🟠 **Pending** - Orange line
- 🟢 **Paid** - Green line
- 🔴 **Cancelled** - Red line

**Use Case**:
- Track payment trends over time
- Identify seasonal patterns
- Monitor receipt completion rates
- Spot issues with cancelled receipts

**Example**:
- January: 5 Draft, 8 Pending, 12 Paid, 2 Cancelled
- February: 3 Draft, 6 Pending, 15 Paid, 1 Cancelled

---

### 4. Receipts by Status (Donut Chart)
**Location**: Bottom Right

**What it shows**: Overall distribution of receipts across different statuses

**Data Source**:
- Counts total receipts in each status
- Shows as percentage of total

**Color Coding**:
- 🔵 **Draft** - Blue
- 🟠 **Pending** - Orange
- 🟢 **Paid** - Green
- 🔴 **Cancelled** - Red

**Use Case**:
- Quick overview of receipt statuses
- Monitor payment collection efficiency
- Identify bottlenecks in payment process

**Example**:
- Paid: 45 receipts (60%)
- Pending: 20 receipts (27%)
- Draft: 8 receipts (11%)
- Cancelled: 2 receipts (2%)

---

## 🔄 Refreshing Charts

### Auto-Refresh
Charts automatically update when you:
- Create a new receipt
- Edit an existing receipt
- Delete a receipt
- Change receipt status

### Manual Refresh
Click the **Refresh button** (🔄) in the Analytics Dashboard header to manually reload all charts.

## 🎯 Getting Started

### If You Have No Data

When you first open the app, you'll see a blue banner:

**"No receipts yet!"**

Click **"Generate Sample Data"** to create 20 sample receipts with:
- Random categories
- Random statuses
- Random dates (last 6 months)
- Random items and amounts

This lets you see how the charts work immediately!

### If You Have Data

You'll see two buttons in the top right:
1. **"Add Sample Data"** - Add 20 more sample receipts
2. **"Clear All Data"** - Delete all receipts (with confirmation)

## 💡 Tips for Better Insights

### 1. Consistent Categorization
Always assign categories to items for accurate charts:
- Use the same category names
- Don't leave categories blank
- Review and standardize periodically

### 2. Update Status Regularly
Keep receipt statuses current:
- Move Draft → Pending when sent
- Move Pending → Paid when received
- Mark Cancelled when appropriate

### 3. Regular Data Review
Check charts weekly or monthly:
- Identify trends early
- Make data-driven decisions
- Adjust strategies as needed

### 4. Clean Old Data
Periodically review and clean:
- Delete test receipts
- Archive old cancelled receipts
- Keep data relevant

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why are my charts empty?**
A: You need to create receipts first. Click "Generate Sample Data" to see charts in action.

**Q: How often do charts update?**
A: Automatically when you create/edit/delete receipts. Click refresh button for manual update.

**Q: Can I export chart data?**
A: Yes! Use "Export CSV" in Receipt List to download all data.

**Q: How far back does monthly data go?**
A: Last 6 months are shown in the monthly trends chart.

**Q: What if I have no items in a category?**
A: That category won't appear in the charts.

**Q: Can I customize chart colors?**
A: Status colors are fixed for consistency. Category colors are auto-assigned.
